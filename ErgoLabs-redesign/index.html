Why ergodicity?

You experience decisions in sequence: what happens today changes what you have tomorrow. A population average answers a different question.

Ergodicity asks when time averages and averages across possible outcomes agree for the same quantity. The coin toss makes that distinction concrete before we introduce the notation.
